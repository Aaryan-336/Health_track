# Product Requirements Document

## Product
A cute, personalised PWA for two partners to track personal health, shared wellness, couple goals, habits, moods, memories and supportive interactions.

## Core Product Goal
Make health tracking feel emotionally warm, collaborative and enjoyable rather than clinical.

## Users
Two connected partners. Each user has private health data and a shared couple space.

## Features

### Personal Health
- Personal health dashboard
- Water tracking
- Meal tracking
- Workout and activity tracking
- Habit tracking
- Mood and stress check-ins
- Journaling
- Daily health score
- Streaks
- Personalised AI insights

### Couple Experience
- Couple health dashboard
- Individual goals
- Shared goals
- Fully custom goals
- Goal categories
- Shared progress tracking
- Couple challenges
- Couple score
- Daily check-in streaks
- Shared wellness updates with consent

### Relationship Goals
- Relationship goals
- Date and adventure goals
- Quality-time goals
- “Our Promise” commitments

### Messaging
- Cute personalised notifications
- Custom messages
- Scheduled reminders
- Full-screen cute message experiences
- Encouragement messages
- Reactions and replies
- “Open When…” letters

### Memories and Celebrations
- Memories and photo timeline
- Couple goal celebrations
- Animated goal completion screens

### Personalisation
- Personalised themes
- Pastel card-based UI
- Abstract organic progress visuals
- Smooth animations
- Dark/light mode
- Profile and privacy controls
- Notification customisation

## Non-Goals for V1
- Medical diagnosis
- Clinical recommendations
- Automatic wearable integration
- Social network features
- Public leaderboards

## Success Criteria
- Two users can securely connect as a couple.
- Both can create personal and shared goals.
- Users can track all selected health metrics.
- Users can send and schedule personalised messages.
- The recipient can receive push notifications after granting permission.
- Shared information respects explicit privacy settings.
