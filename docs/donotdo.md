# Do Not Do

1. Do not turn the app into a public social network.
2. Do not automatically share health information with a partner.
3. Do not create a surveillance-style partner dashboard.
4. Do not present AI insights as medical facts.
5. Do not diagnose, prescribe or claim clinical certainty.
6. Do not overfill every screen with hearts, stickers and decorative objects.
7. Do not use bright saturated colours everywhere.
8. Do not copy the exact UI of another product; use the supplied references only as visual inspiration.
9. Do not use generic dashboard cards everywhere; preserve asymmetry and editorial composition.
10. Do not put business logic inside React components.
11. Do not calculate sensitive scores only on the client.
12. Do not send notifications without permission.
13. Do not spam reminders.
14. Do not store push provider secrets in the frontend.
15. Do not expose raw device tokens to other users.
16. Do not allow one user to accept a promise on behalf of both users.
17. Do not mark a shared goal complete without checking the server-side rule.
18. Do not assume a PWA can fully customise the operating system notification tray.
19. Do not make missing tracking data automatically equal failure.
20. Do not build wearable integrations, medical records or advanced calorie AI into the first scope.
